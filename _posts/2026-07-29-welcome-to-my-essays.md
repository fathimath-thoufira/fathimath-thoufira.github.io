This is a sample essay to show you how posts work.

Every essay you write goes into the `_posts` folder as a file named like this:

```
YYYY-MM-DD-a-short-title.md
```

The date in the filename controls the order they appear in (newest first), and the "title" part becomes part of the web address for that essay.

At the top of every file, between the `---` lines, you set:
- **title** — shown at the top of the essay and in the essay list
- **date** — used for sorting
- **categories** — optional, lets you group essays by topic
- **excerpt** — a short teaser shown in the essay list

Everything below the second `---` is just your essay, written in plain text using **Markdown** — so:

- `**bold**` becomes **bold**
- `*italic*` becomes *italic*
- `## Heading` becomes a heading
- A blank line starts a new paragraph

That's it — write your essay like a normal document, save it as a new file in `_posts`, and it'll show up automatically on your Essays page.
